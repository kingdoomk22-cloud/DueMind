WHY DUEMIND

Not another invoice scheduler.

It is designed around the messy part: knowing what to say next, when to wait, and when a client has actually promised a payment date.

01

Today view

See only the invoices that need action now instead of living inside a complicated accounting dashboard.

02

Adaptive follow-up

Drafts shift from friendly to professional, firm, or final based on how late the invoice is.

03

Promise tracking

Record simple replies like “I’ll pay Friday,” pause the chase, then surface the invoice again if the date passes.

04

Approval mode

Keep a human in control. Review, edit, or skip each message before it is recorded.

05

Simple imports

The prototype supports manual invoice entry now; CSV and PDF import are the next production step.

06

Built for small teams

Focused on freelancers, consultants, and agencies that care more about getting paid than learning another ERP.
